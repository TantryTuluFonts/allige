# FONTLOG for Allige

Allige is an open-source font for Tulu lipi, designed by Prahlad Prasad Tantry and
released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `OFL.txt`).

A TrueType font (Academy edition); the logo is in `images/`.
